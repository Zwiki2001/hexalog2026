FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before placing your first order with HexaLog.

Can't find your answer? Send us a message and we'll get back to you within a few hours.

info@hexalog.eu
Our pricing is based on route, pallet dimensions, weight, and order type (B2B or B2C). When you fill out the booking form, our system calculates an instant quote. B2B shipments (business addresses) are generally cheaper because drivers can access loading docks and there is no residential surcharge.
A pallet is a flat transport structure on which goods are stacked. We accept the standard sizes:

Euro pallet: 120 × 80 cm (max 1,000 kg)
Block pallet: 120 × 100 cm (max 1,200 kg)
Half pallet: 80 × 60 cm (max 500 kg)

Maximum height with goods is 220 cm. For oversized or heavy shipments, please contact us directly.
All our current routes are within the EU (Netherlands, Belgium, Luxembourg, France), so there are no customs requirements. Goods move freely within the single market. We provide a digital CMR (transport document) with every shipment, which is available to download from your client portal.
After booking, you receive an Order ID and a confirmation email. You can track your shipment anytime using the tracking bar at the top of this page — just enter your Order ID and email. You will see the current status: Pending → Confirmed → Picked Up → In Transit → Delivered.
B2B (Business to Business) — delivery to a company address, warehouse, or shop. Usually cheaper and faster since someone is always present to receive the goods.

B2C (Business to Consumer) — delivery to a private home address. Requires a phone number so the driver can coordinate. May include a residential surcharge.
Standard delivery within the Benelux region is 24–48 hours after pickup. Deliveries to France typically take 48–72 hours depending on the destination city. Express options may be available — contact us for urgent shipments.
You can cancel or change your order as long as it has not yet been picked up. Please contact us as soon as possible at info@hexalog.eu. Once the shipment is in transit, changes cannot be guaranteed.
We send your invoice after successful delivery. You can pay by bank transfer or PayPal. All payment details are included on the invoice, which you can download directly from your client portal. Payment term is 14 days from delivery date.
